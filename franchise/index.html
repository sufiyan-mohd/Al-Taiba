Common Questions

Franchise FAQ

How can I apply for an Al-Taiba franchise?

To apply for an Al-Taiba franchise, simply call our team directly at 6366646009. We will discuss the franchise process and help you get started with the right package.

What is the cost of an Al-Taiba franchise?

Both our Shop Franchise and Food Cart Franchise packages require a total setup investment of ₹7 Lakhs. There are no hidden fees in the initial setup package listed.

What are the requirements to open an Al-Taiba franchise?

Requirements depend on the format you choose. The total setup fee includes essential equipment like 4 fryer machines, raw materials like secret masala, and specific branding for either a physical shop or a mobile food cart.

Is the Al-Taiba franchise available in Bangalore?

Yes! Al-Taiba Crispy Chicken Center currently operates popular branches in Kanakanagar and Shivajinagar. We are actively expanding our food franchise opportunities across Bangalore and surrounding areas.
